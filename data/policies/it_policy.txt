IT USAGE & SECURITY POLICY
Company: GoAified Technologies Pvt. Ltd.
Version: 3.0 | Effective: January 1, 2026
Document Owner: IT Department

─────────────────────────────────────────
1. PURPOSE & SCOPE
─────────────────────────────────────────
This policy governs acceptable use of all IT systems, devices, software, and networks owned or operated by GoAified Technologies. It applies to all employees, contractors, interns, and third-party vendors with access to company resources.

─────────────────────────────────────────
2. DEVICE & EQUIPMENT POLICY
─────────────────────────────────────────

2.1 Company-Issued Devices
- All company laptops, phones, and peripherals remain the property of GoAified.
- Devices must be returned upon resignation, termination, or role change within 2 working days.
- Employees are responsible for physical care of assigned equipment.
- Loss or damage must be reported to IT within 24 hours via the IT helpdesk portal.
- Personal use of company devices is permitted for incidental purposes only (e.g., checking personal email briefly). Heavy personal use is prohibited.

2.2 BYOD (Bring Your Own Device)
- Personal devices may be used to access company email and collaboration tools only after enrolling in Mobile Device Management (MDM).
- Jailbroken or rooted devices may not be enrolled in MDM.
- IT reserves the right to remotely wipe company data from enrolled personal devices upon termination.

─────────────────────────────────────────
3. SOFTWARE & LICENSING
─────────────────────────────────────────
- Only IT-approved software may be installed on company devices. Approved software list: software-catalog.goaified.com.
- Employees must not use unlicensed or pirated software on any company device.
- Software installation requests must be raised via an IT ticket. Approval typically takes 1–2 business days.
- Cloud/SaaS tool subscriptions must be procured through IT to ensure security review and billing centralization.

─────────────────────────────────────────
4. NETWORK & INTERNET ACCESS
─────────────────────────────────────────
- Company WiFi (SSID: GoAified-Corp) is for business use. Guests must use the GoAified-Guest network.
- VPN (GlobalProtect) must be used for all remote access to internal systems.
- VPN credentials are issued during onboarding and expire every 90 days. Renewal is initiated through IT helpdesk.
- Prohibited internet activities: streaming for personal entertainment, torrenting, accessing gambling or adult content sites, cryptocurrency mining.
- All internet traffic on the corporate network is monitored and logged for security compliance.

─────────────────────────────────────────
5. DATA SECURITY & CONFIDENTIALITY
─────────────────────────────────────────
- All sensitive company data must be stored in approved company storage (Google Drive or company servers).
- Employees must not store company data on personal cloud storage (e.g., personal Dropbox, iCloud).
- Confidential data must be shared only via encrypted channels (email with encryption or company Slack).
- Employees must not share passwords, login credentials, or access tokens with colleagues.
- Password requirements: Minimum 12 characters, at least one uppercase, lowercase, digit, and special character. Must be rotated every 90 days.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all company accounts.

─────────────────────────────────────────
6. ACCESS MANAGEMENT
─────────────────────────────────────────
- Access to systems is provisioned on a need-to-know, least-privilege basis.
- New hire access provisioning is completed within 2 business days of joining.
- Access requests for additional tools or permissions must be submitted via IT helpdesk and approved by the requesting employee's manager.
- Access is revoked within 4 hours of an employee's last working day being confirmed.
- Privileged access (admin rights) is reviewed quarterly.

─────────────────────────────────────────
7. INCIDENT REPORTING
─────────────────────────────────────────
- Security incidents (phishing, malware, data breach, unauthorized access) must be reported to security@goaified.com immediately.
- IT helpdesk tickets must be raised for all hardware/software issues.
- SLA for ticket response:
  - Critical: 1 hour response, 4 hour resolution
  - High: 4 hour response, 8 hour resolution
  - Medium: 8 hour response, 24 hour resolution
  - Low: 24 hour response, 72 hour resolution

─────────────────────────────────────────
9. CONSEQUENCES OF VIOLATIONS
─────────────────────────────────────────
- Minor violations: Verbal/written warning.
- Moderate violations: Formal disciplinary action, potential salary hold.
- Severe violations (data breach, unauthorized access, software piracy): Immediate termination and potential legal action.
