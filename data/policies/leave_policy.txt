LEAVE & ATTENDANCE POLICY
Company: GoAified Technologies Pvt. Ltd.
Version: 2.1 | Effective: January 1, 2026
Document Owner: HR Department

─────────────────────────────────────────
1. OVERVIEW
─────────────────────────────────────────
This policy governs all leave entitlements, application procedures, and attendance requirements for all full-time employees at GoAified Technologies.

─────────────────────────────────────────
2. LEAVE TYPES & ENTITLEMENTS
─────────────────────────────────────────

2.1 Annual Leave (Earned Leave)
- Entitlement: 18 days per calendar year for employees with less than 5 years of service.
- Entitlement: 21 days per calendar year for employees with 5 or more years of service.
- Accrual: 1.5 days per month. Leave is credited on the 1st of each month.
- Carry Forward: Up to 10 unused annual leave days may be carried forward to the next year. Any excess is forfeited.
- Encashment: Up to 5 days of annual leave can be encashed annually during the month of March, subject to manager approval.
- Application: Annual leave must be applied at least 3 working days in advance, except in emergencies.

2.2 Sick Leave
- Entitlement: 12 days per calendar year.
- Usage: Sick leave may be taken for personal illness, injury, or medical appointments.
- Medical Certificate: A medical certificate from a registered practitioner is required for sick leave exceeding 3 consecutive days.
- No carry forward: Unused sick leave lapses at the end of the calendar year.

2.3 Casual Leave
- Entitlement: 6 days per calendar year.
- Usage: For short-term personal needs or emergencies. Cannot be taken for more than 3 consecutive days.
- Application: Should be applied in advance wherever possible.
- No carry forward or encashment: Unused casual leave lapses at year end.

2.4 Maternity Leave
- Entitlement: 26 weeks (182 days) of paid leave for the first two children, as per the Maternity Benefit Act, 1961 (amended 2017).
- For third child onward: 12 weeks of paid leave.
- Adoption: Female employees adopting a child below 3 months receive 12 weeks of paid leave.
- Application: Must be submitted at least 4 weeks in advance wherever possible.

2.5 Paternity Leave
- Entitlement: 10 working days of paid leave.
- Must be availed within 6 months of the child's birth or adoption.

2.6 Bereavement Leave
- Entitlement: 5 working days for immediate family (spouse, children, parents, siblings).
- 3 working days for extended family (grandparents, in-laws).

2.7 Compensatory Off (Comp-Off)
- Earned when an employee works on a designated holiday or weekend, with prior manager approval.
- Must be availed within 60 days of earning.

─────────────────────────────────────────
3. LEAVE APPLICATION PROCEDURE
─────────────────────────────────────────
1. Log into the HRMS portal (hrms.goaified.com).
2. Navigate to Leave > Apply Leave.
3. Select leave type, dates, and add a reason.
4. Submit for manager approval.
5. Managers must approve or reject within 2 working days.
6. Emergency leave can be applied retroactively within 1 working day.

─────────────────────────────────────────
4. ATTENDANCE & WORKING HOURS
─────────────────────────────────────────
- Core working hours: 10:00 AM – 7:00 PM IST, Monday to Friday.
- Minimum billable hours: 8 hours per working day.
- Flexi-time: Employees may start between 9:00 AM – 11:00 AM, provided 8 hours are completed.
- Remote work: Up to 2 days per week for roles designated as hybrid.
- Late arrival: Arriving more than 30 minutes after scheduled start time twice in a month is treated as half-day leave.

─────────────────────────────────────────
5. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS
─────────────────────────────────────────
GoAified observes 11 national/regional public holidays per year as announced by HR at the start of the year. The 2026 holiday calendar is available on the HRMS portal.

─────────────────────────────────────────
6. LEAVE WITHOUT PAY (LWP)
─────────────────────────────────────────
- Leave without pay is granted only after all applicable leave balances are exhausted.
- Requires VP-level approval for more than 5 consecutive days.
- LWP impacts annual increments and bonus calculations on a pro-rata basis.

─────────────────────────────────────────
7. POLICY VIOLATIONS
─────────────────────────────────────────
- Taking leave without approval may be treated as Leave Without Pay.
- Falsifying leave records is a disciplinary offence and may lead to termination.
