CODE OF CONDUCT & HR POLICY
Company: GoAified Technologies Pvt. Ltd.
Version: 2.0 | Effective: January 1, 2026
Document Owner: HR Department

─────────────────────────────────────────
1. WORKPLACE BEHAVIOUR
─────────────────────────────────────────
All employees are expected to:
- Treat colleagues, clients, and stakeholders with respect and professionalism.
- Foster an inclusive, harassment-free work environment.
- Avoid conflicts of interest and disclose any potential conflicts to HR immediately.
- Protect confidential company and client information.

─────────────────────────────────────────
2. ANTI-HARASSMENT & DISCRIMINATION POLICY
─────────────────────────────────────────
GoAified has a zero-tolerance policy toward:
- Harassment of any kind (verbal, physical, sexual, digital).
- Discrimination based on gender, race, religion, caste, disability, age, sexual orientation, or nationality.

Reporting: Employees may report incidents to hr@goaified.com or use the anonymous reporting tool on the HRMS portal. All reports are investigated within 7 working days.

─────────────────────────────────────────
3. PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT
─────────────────────────────────────────
- Performance reviews are conducted bi-annually (June and December).
- Annual increments are effective from April 1 each year.
- Employees on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) are reviewed every 30 days.
- Promotions are recommended by managers and approved by HR and the relevant VP.

─────────────────────────────────────────
4. COMPENSATION & PAYROLL
─────────────────────────────────────────
- Salaries are credited on the last working day of each month.
- Pay slips are available on the HRMS portal by the 2nd of each month.
- Salary revision letters are shared by April 15 for the new financial year.
- Reimbursements (travel, food, equipment) must be submitted within 30 days of incurring the expense via the HRMS portal. 

─────────────────────────────────────────
5. ONBOARDING
─────────────────────────────────────────
- New employees receive a Welcome Kit and onboarding checklist on Day 1.
- System access provisioning: completed within 2 business days.
- Buddy program: Each new hire is assigned a buddy from their team for the first 30 days.
- Probation period: 3 months for all new hires. Performance is reviewed at the end of probation.

─────────────────────────────────────────
6. SEPARATION & OFFBOARDING
─────────────────────────────────────────
- Notice period: 60 days for all employees (may be bought out with HR approval).
- Exit interview is conducted by HR within the last 2 weeks.
- Full and Final settlement is processed within 45 days of the last working day.
- Company equipment must be returned before the last working day.
- Access to all company systems is revoked on the last working day.

─────────────────────────────────────────
7. TRAVEL & EXPENSE POLICY
─────────────────────────────────────────
- Domestic air travel: Economy class only (Business class permitted for journeys > 6 hours with VP approval).
- Hotel: Up to ₹5,000 per night for metro cities; ₹3,500 for non-metro.
- Local transport: Reimbursed via cab receipts; personal vehicle reimbursed at ₹8/km.
- Daily allowance (DA): ₹700/day for metro; ₹500/day for non-metro.
- All expenses must have original receipts and must be claimed within 30 days.

─────────────────────────────────────────
8. HEALTH & WELLNESS BENEFITS
─────────────────────────────────────────
- Group medical insurance: ₹5,00,000 per employee and dependents (spouse + 2 children).
- Mental health: 4 free counselling sessions per year via the EAP (Employee Assistance Program). Contact: eap@goaified.com.
- Gym reimbursement: Up to ₹2,000/month upon submission of gym membership receipt.
- Annual health check-up: Fully covered for employees above 35 years; 50% covered for others.
